Kim Dong Hyuk is an interdisciplinary filmmaker, researcher, and designer working at the intersection of computational technology and applied design. He is currently pursuing his M.S. at the Design Informatics Lab (DILAB), Hanyang University, where he researches the application of AI in Design and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), developing methodologies that bridge data-driven analysis with creative workflows.

His paper, Behavior-Aware Anthropometric Scene Generation for Human-Usable 3D Layouts, presented at CHI 2026 in Barcelona, examines how AI systems can generate 3D spaces that account for human body use and movement. Drawing from this, his research interests broadly center on how space is represented, generated, and inhabited, spanning cinematography, 3D spatial computing, and embodied interaction.

His background in film and visual storytelling informs his research foundation. Dong Hyuk's work in filmmaking has been invited to and awarded at numerous international film festivals; he also produces occasional campaign projects for clients such as Adidas and the Korea National Contemporary Dance Company.
