Kim Dong Hyuk is an interdisciplinary filmmaker, researcher, and designer working at the intersection of computational technology and applied design. He is currently pursuing his M.S. at the Design Informatics Lab (DILAB), Hanyang University, where he researches the application of AI in Design and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), developing methodologies that bridge data-driven analysis with creative workflows.

His research is reflected in recent publications. BehaviorAware, a paper at CHI 2026, was presented in Barcelona; his position paper was also selected for the CHI 2026 workshop on LLM Simulacra. He has developed a multimodal AI pipeline that applies computational methodologies to Cinematics, enabling a quantitative analysis of how shot compositions create emotional shifts, and explores 3D pre-visualization for film storyboarding alongside narrative scenario generation through generative AI tools.

His primary focus on film and visual storytelling informs his research foundation. Dong Hyuk's work in filmmaking has been invited to and awarded at numerous international film festivals. In parallel, he contributes his visual and directorial skills to campaign projects for clients such as Adidas and the Korea National Contemporary Dance Company. Grounded in his studies in Film, Architectural Design and AI, Dong Hyuk consistently seeks to create new things for compelling experiences.
