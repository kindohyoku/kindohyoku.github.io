kim donghyuk

kim donghyuk is a filmmaker, researcher, and designer working between computational technology and applied design.

He is pursuing his M.S. at the Design Informatics Lab, Hanyang University, researching how AI systems generate and evaluate spaces that human bodies can actually use — presented at CHI 2026 in Barcelona.

His films have been invited to international festivals; his design and campaign work reaches audiences through brands and stages.
