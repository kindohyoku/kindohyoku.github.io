Kim Dong Hyuk

Kim Dong Hyuk is a filmmaker, researcher, and designer working between computational technology and applied design.

At the Design Informatics Lab, Hanyang University, he researches how AI systems can generate and evaluate spaces that human bodies actually use — work recently presented at CHI 2026 in Barcelona.

His films have been invited to international festivals; his design and campaign work reaches audiences through brands and stages.
